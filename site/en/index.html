01 Features

One button.
Six reasons to press it.

Recording, screenshots, sound and region markup. No heavy editor, no extra windows.

01

Screen and camera recording

The whole screen, one window or a selected region. MKV, up to 2160p and 60 FPS; the mouse cursor can be shown or hidden. Camera photo and video are included.

MKV up to 2160p 60 FPS Camera
02

Screenshots

The screen, a source or a region — saved as PNG with one press. Copy the file straight away or open its folder.

PNG Screen Region
03

GIF and WebP

A short animation for a chat, an article or a bug report. Presets and a frame-rate control.

GIF WebP Loop
04

Sound

System sound and the microphone together, with a separate mic level. Audio only is fine too.

System Microphone MP3
05

Region editor

Draw an arrow, add text, crop or cover something right on the selection. Save it, copy it or start recording.

Annotations Redaction
06

Lives in the tray

Click the icon and capture starts. Recent files are at hand: open, copy or show in the folder.

Tray History Clipboard

Local.
No account.
No cloud.

Capture Button writes files straight to disk. No subscriptions, no profiles and no uploads to a server — just the capture and the finished file.

Nothing in the way.
